# Math-for-Spatial-Audio

> **"Bridging the gap between abstract Linear Algebra and immersive sound experiences."**

This repository is a dedicated log of my journey to master the mathematical foundations required for **Spatial Audio Engineering**. My ultimate goal is to develop the world's best spatial audio technology, recreating the raw emotion of a live concert through digital streaming.

## 🚀 Mission Statement

As a student and a soldier currently serving in the military, I am committed to daily consistent growth. I believe that a deep understanding of **Linear Algebra** is the bedrock of Digital Signal Processing (DSP) and Spatial Audio modeling.
